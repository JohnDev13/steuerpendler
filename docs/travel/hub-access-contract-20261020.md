## User-facing rule

A municipality has a readable **reference place** and **local main stop**, not a mandatory first boarding point. A person in Staufen may walk directly to Lenzburg station or walk to a local bus stop, take a bus to Lenzburg and change to a train. The choice depends on the residential point and the morning services. Niederlenz may show its civic centre/Rathaus as a place label and Dorfplatz as a local stop; these are not interchangeable coordinates. Lenzburg's rail station is its rail reference. A person's exact address is not known to the municipality-level product: use the evidenced STATPOP population-weighted point for a clearly labelled indicative estimate and test inhabited near/far cells separately in the pilot. Never claim that one centre-based result describes every address.

Find nearby service-active local stops **and** out-of-municipality hub entrances by a verified OSM pedestrian path. Candidate walk time is external access from the residential point to the stop/station entrance; it is not a walk to a precise boarding platform. A label grouping co-located bus and rail stops does **not** fuse their GTFS IDs or grant instantaneous transfer. Within a transit itinerary, use applicable directed published transfer constraints, e.g. the selected 2026-10-17 Swiss GTFS has a 240-second Lenzburg bus-bay→rail minimum; count subsequent waiting as transit. Unknown or forbidden transfers are not zero. Do not add a separate origin entrance-to-platform time or a generic "finding the platform" penalty.

Enumerate actual scheduled first-board events in inclusive `[06:30:00,08:30:00]` Europe/Zurich on the frozen, independently approved Tuesday. Exclude pre-first-board waiting at home and external access from *transit* elapsed seconds. For each feasible itinerary retain external access-walk seconds and first-board→destination-arrival seconds separately. Count transfer walks and waits in the latter. At the illustrative Aarau pilot limits, require ceil(walkSeconds/60) ≤15 and ceil(transitSeconds/60) ≤20 independently. The existing count-walk switch additionally applies `ceil(walkSeconds/60)+ceil(transitSeconds/60) ≤ maxTime` when on. Among eligible alternatives prefer least exact active time `walkSeconds+transitSeconds`, then least transitSeconds, walkSeconds, earliest first board, stable stop IDs. Retain the nondominated walk/transit alternatives per home/workplace so changing sliders cannot accidentally discard a shorter-walk route. Do not emit invented exact departures from `frequencies.exact_times=0` or approximate stop times.

## Product shape and migration gate

Target release data schema is **v2**, with per-Gemeinde representative point, independently named reference stop and candidate walking access; per-workplace rows carry multiple evidenced alternatives (external walk seconds, transit seconds, origin/destination stop IDs, first-board/arrival seconds). Details and fail-closed rules are in `SPEC.md` §§6.3–6.5, 8. The shipped `src/filter.ts` and travel primitives still implement the older one-stop v1 schema. Until parsers, frontend filters, source/validation manifests and tests all migrate coherently, leave `site/data/manifest.json` travel unavailable. Do not create hybrid v1/v2 assets or silently cast an option to a single `[homeBfs, minutes]` row. The main-stop label is not an origin constraint. Preserve all 2,110 residential municipalities, including non-capitals and neighbouring cantons, against the existing 19 selected workplace-capital destinations; a four-canton all-to-all matrix is a diagnostic only.
